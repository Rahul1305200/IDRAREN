Who we are

A small team, and a lot of looms.

Talk to a designer →

We work directly with weaving families across the Middle and High Atlas. People we know by name, in workshops we visit. No middlemen, no factory floor. Every rug we sell was made by hands we can point to.

And you are not left to choose alone. Our designers stay with you from the first question to the finished piece. And if nothing in the collection is quite right for your room, we will have one made for you.
