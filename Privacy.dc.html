Privacy Policy

How Idraren handles the information you share with the house.

What we collect

When you enquire or subscribe, we may collect your name, email, message content, language preference, and basic technical data needed to run the site.

How we use it

To reply to enquiries, prepare quotations, operate trade conversations, and, if you opt in, send occasional letters from the Atlas. We do not sell your data.

Storage & processors

Data may be processed with trusted email and hosting providers. We keep enquiry records only as long as needed for the conversation and reasonable business records.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold. Write to hello@idraren.com.

Updates

We may update this policy; the date of change will appear here when revised. This page is a working draft pending counsel review.
